## English Version

Today I reorganized my understanding of the relationship between the VLN project and the vehicle project. VLN itself is an independent module. Previously, I looked at it together with chassis control and the `twist` bridge mainly to understand how it connects to the vehicle system. Now the project structure and responsibility boundaries are clearer: chassis control and the `twist` bridge already have existing implementations, and they are not the main difficulty at this stage. Therefore, future work should not focus too much on low-level motion control.

The more important direction now is the upper-level VLN capability. First, I need to work on the implementation and optimization of the end-to-end model, and understand the relationship between image input, language instructions, history information, and action output. Second, I need to continue studying the camera node, especially how images are captured from the camera, published to ROS2 topics, and then passed into the inference pipeline. Third, I need to read papers to understand the difficulty of high-level decision-making in VLN, including how scene understanding, target recognition, path selection, and action generation are connected.

Future work should focus on model performance, visual input, language instructions, and action output. In the short term, I should first understand the full pipeline from camera input to remote inference and action output. In the medium term, I should combine paper reading with model structure analysis to decide which parts of the current NaVIDA / Qwen-RobotNav route need adaptation. In the long term, I can consider fine-tuning based on the actual model performance.
